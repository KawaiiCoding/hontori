## The chapter (light and dark, levels 28 to 30)
- After the Halo Ring (`c9:complete`) the stair to the Vael Athenaeum opens above Goldmere. It is open only to the tournament winner. Merrit's sketch from chapter 8 shows the way past the locked landing (a short payoff).
- Places: the Athenaeum Stair (a long climb with one rest landing halfway: ramen from a kind attendant and a bed), the Athenaeum (a grand hall, three Council chambers, Vael's study), and Vael's garden of jars (chapter 9) seen again at the end.
- Three Council duels, each under one OLD rule from the journey (owner design: the Council uses the rules it took from the schools): `noSwitch`, `noRest`, `blind`. Each Councillor has a name, a look and two or three hontori. A save point is before each duel, and a lost duel restarts at that duel.
- Grand Archmage Orlen Vael (dark and light), with 5 hontori. He fights in TWO PHASES. NEW rule `seal`: at the start of each of his turns he seals one of the kid's loadout spells, one at a time, until only the V is left. Phase two begins at half health with one short line. The kid wins with the first spell: the V is never sealed, and drawing it in phase two weakens his shield (the library decides the exact effect; it must be clear on screen).
- THE GREAT V (new command `greatV`): after the duel, every Wander the kid met stands in the hall: Mira, Cole, Wren, Pim, the Wardens, Merrit, Lisel, Mom, Dad. Each draws a stroke, and the kid draws the last V across the whole screen; it lifts the veil over the city. The city is asked first (rule 71: only the willing see).
- MERCY, the real ending: Vael is defeated, then offered mercy. The kid asks him whether he wants to see. He was afraid of what he could not keep safe (rule 104). Yes or no is the PLAYER's choice, flag `c10:mercy` or `c10:nomercy`; Mira's last scene and the ending lines depend on it.
- THE NEW LAW: the kid writes it. Eight votes (the rings) plus the last ring. The player picks one line from a small set (flag `c10:law_*`), and it is shown as the law's new first line. The hidden last line (rule 105) is undone.
- Vael's garden: the jars are opened with the V, and the sleeping hontori wake and go home (a calm scene, the last sight before the credits).
- Ending: a short picture story (reuse the opening's PictureStory: 5 or 6 panels mirroring the opening, colour returning), then credits with the people and hontori, then home: Mom, Dad, Grandma Ida's wand on the mantel, the fox by the fire.
- AFTER THE END (free roam, the game never locks):
  - the Emberbrook fair at last (stalls; coins buy keepsakes: hats, a picture frame, a lantern for the home);
  - 16 rare hontori that appear only after the end, to fill the wand memory to 100;
  - rematches with every Warden;
  - a final lore book.
- Rewards: the Archmage's Ring (`archmage_ring`, key), a "Wander's Charter" key item (`charter`), and the last evolution of the kid's hontori if it is not there yet.
