## Lessons from chapter 3 (apply from the start; most of chapter 3's polish came from these)
1. No stretch longer than about 3 minutes without a reward, a person, a find or a short scene; a long road gets a lookout or a rest spot halfway.
2. Every puzzle piece is big, clearly drawn, in an open spot, with a visible effect when used, and one short helper card at first need.
3. Every item and spell says in plain words what it does, where the player sees it.
4. Nothing is ever placed on water or walls; the content checker must stay at zero problems.
5. A small school is dressed as a school (map `school: { door, element, name, touch }`), never a plain house.
6. Rest is near every boss; a skipped teacher can be reached again without a long walk.
7. Nothing waits on weather or time of day for the main path.
8. Effects only with a visible cause; timed text uses readMs.

## Chapter 4: The Bell Under the Tide (water, levels 11 to 14)
- The ferry from Kitewick's lake south to the coast (a boat ride scene, no new mechanic beyond a fade and a short crossing) reaches Tidewell, a harbour town. Places: Tidewell (tavern with ramen and bed, shop, about 4 houses), Saltmarsh (reeds and boardwalks), the Sunken Stair (a tidal path down to the drowned bell).
- Small school: the Lullaby Boat (a moored boat dressed as a school) teaches the Sleep Sigil (spell id `sleep`, Dream Veil).
- Great school: Deepbell Hall, Warden Maren, Apprentice Isk. SHAPE BREAK: the hall is flooded and closed; Maren will not duel until the spirit bell is raised. The quest comes first: three tide wheels (puzzle type `tide`: each wheel raises or lowers the water in one basin; low water opens a path) let the player walk the Sunken Stair and free the bell. Ring rule `tideRise`: each round the field floods a little; fire grows weaker and water stronger each round, so finish fast. Drawing twist `mirror`: the guide is shown mirrored in the water. The Tide Ring is the third Warden's vote.
- Story: Orlen Vael's name is on the old order that sank the bell so no one would hear hontori sing; a warm letter of praise from him arrives by kite the same day. PAYOFF: Rook the ferryman's "lost little cry" was the bell's spirit (Rook is in Emberbrook's tavern; a kite letter from him, or he rides the ferry). Mira is from Tidewell; her mother Lisel is afraid to see. Mira wants her to stop being afraid; the V only works if she is willing (rule 71): the kid asks, Lisel is not ready yet (pays off in chapter 9). Mira rematch with 3 hontori.
- Coins: ferry ticket (first crossing free with Hask's word), a better hook for night fish, Tide Pearls cheap.

## Chapter 5: The City That Never Sleeps (electric, levels 14 to 17)
- From Tidewell a coast road north to Coilhaven, a workshop city of copper roofs and wires. Places: Coilhaven (tavern, shop with higher prices, about 4 houses, the square), Sparkmoor (a wet moor with lightning rods), the Storm Works (factory school).
- Small school: the Tinker's Shed teaches the Stun Sigil (spell id `stun`, Thunder Seal).
- Great school: the Storm Works, Warden Volta Quill, Apprentice Pim. Entrance test: route power through switch tiles to light the door (puzzle type `circuit`: tiles you rotate to connect a power line from a source to the door). Ring rule `noRest`: three duels in a row (Pim, a foreman, Volta), no bed, no ramen, no teddy between them, only items carried (a save point between duels so a loss restarts at the current duel, never from zero). Drawing twist `quick`: each sigil must be finished before a spark runs down a wire drawn beside the page (generous time; the guide stays). The Spark Ring is the fourth Warden's vote.
- Story: Coilhaven's lamps burn on hontori trapped in lamp jars, sold as "tame sparks" (rule 81). The kid frees one lamp's spark (a Zappity) in a quiet scene. THE CHOICE (remembered later, flag `c5:spoke` or `c5:quiet`): speak out in the square, or stay quiet as the Elder advised. Speak out: the workers remember you, but grey cloaks block the next road (a short duel with two cloaks on the way out). Stay quiet: the road is easy, but Pim remembers you said nothing. PAYOFF: the Archmage's Token glows near every grey cloak; it was telling them where the kid is (the kid can keep it or drop it in the river; a choice flag `c5:token`). Cole's father Bram Ashfield is a ring-smith here who sells the jars and does not know what is inside; Cole arrives, sees, and argues with him (no duel; one short scene).
- Coins: prices are higher here; herbs and snacks matter before the gauntlet.
