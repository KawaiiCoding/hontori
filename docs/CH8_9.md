## Chapter 8: The Town With No Lamps (dark, levels 23 to 25)
- From Cinderreach (on `c7:complete`) the dark Mothwood leads to Duskmere, a town where it is always evening (time stays at dusk inside its maps). Places: the Mothwood (dark, moths of light), Duskmere (tavern with ramen and bed, shop that trades favours more than coins, about 4 houses), Hollow Moon (great school in a crater). The lantern must carry a Cinderreach flame to enter the Mothwood (flag from chapter 7).
- Small school: the Lantern Room teaches the Lantern Sigil (new spell id `lantern`: in battle it shows what the foe plans for the next two turns, even under the Blind rule).
- Great school: Hollow Moon, Warden Nox Ardley. Entrance test: slip past the grey watchers (puzzle type `watchers`: patrolling grey cloaks with light cones; stay out of the cones, hide in shadows; caught means a gentle reset to the last shadow, never a fight). SHAPE BREAK: no apprentice duel; instead a Council captain attacks the town and Nox fights beside the kid (a double battle: the kid's lead and Nox's hontori against the captain's two; a new battle mode `ally`). Ring rule `blind`: the foe's plan is hidden and one of the four spell slots is sealed for the duel. Drawing twist `unseen`: no line is shown while drawing, only the result. The Moon Ring is the seventh Warden's vote.
- Story: everyone is told dark spirits are the bad ones; Duskmere hides the hontori the Council hunts. Nox says it aloud: Vael is afraid of what he cannot keep safe. PAYOFF: the grey cloak from Mossgrove (chapter 2) changes sides, breaks the Sanctum seal (flag `c8:unseal`, the hontori wake and return), and knows a way up the Athenaeum stair (plant for chapter 10). Mira's mother Lisel writes: she is ready to see (payoff in chapter 9). The last evolution (level 25) happens in this chapter.
- Coins: little to buy; forest finds from other lands sell for more here.

## Chapter 9: The Golden City (light, levels 25 to 28)
- The golden gate of Goldmere opens to seven rings. Places: Goldmere (the capital: a grand plaza, the best shop in the game with something worth a few gold, a tavern, about 4 houses, Vael's garden), the Mirror Gardens, Halo Court (great school and the tournament arena).
- Small school: the Glasshouse teaches the Mirror Sigil (new spell id `mirror`: sends a status effect back to the foe).
- Great school: Halo Court, Warden Aurel, Apprentice Lys. Entrance test: the Mirror Gardens (puzzle type `mirrors`: turn mirrors to bounce a beam of light to the gate). The trial is the Ring Tournament (a new `tournament` mode: four rounds in a row against ring holders, Cole and Mira among them, with a bracket screen between rounds and a save point at each). Rule `pure`: no binding and no items. Drawing twist `flawless`: in the final round only Flawless sigils take effect. The Halo Ring is the eighth Warden's vote: with it, all eight votes are the kid's.
- Story: the city adores Vael. He hosts the kid kindly, in person, all chapter long. He offers Cole an apprenticeship; Cole must choose between the offer and what he saw, and chooses in the final round (he stands with the kid). PAYOFF: the chapter 5 choice decides who stands up in the crowd (`c5:spoke`: Coilhaven's workers; `c5:quiet`: only Pim). PAYOFF: Mom and Dad arrive to watch and they can see, which is against his law; Mira's mother Lisel sees for the first time (the V, willingly). PLANT: Vael's garden of jars (sleeping hontori, the final secret).
- Coins: the best shop: something worth a few gold.
