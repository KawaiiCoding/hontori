## Chapter 6: What the Stones Remember (earth, levels 17 to 20)
- From Coilhaven's north gate (it opens on `c5:complete`) a mountain road climbs to Stonehollow, a cliff town of carved stone. Places: Stonehollow (tavern with ramen and bed, shop, about 4 houses), the Old Quarry, the Listening Cave (dark tunnels; the Lantern from home matters here: without it the cave shows only a small circle around the kid).
- Small school: the Drum Circle (an open stone ring with drums, dressed as a school) teaches the Rally Sigil (spell id `boost`, Rally Rune).
- Great school: Bedrock Hall, Warden Harrow, Apprentice Dunn (Dunn exists in ch3 as a person id; reuse `dunn` if it fits, else `dunn_hollow`). Entrance test: the dark tunnels with boulders (reuse the push puzzle type from the Root Maze with darkness and the lantern). Ring rule `wandDuel`: WAND TO WAND, a caster duel with no hontori on the field: the player and Harrow each draw sigils; wards block, stuns skip a turn, strikes hurt the caster's focus. The Stone Ring is the fifth Warden's vote.
- Story: cave carvings show people and hontori side by side: the old law is only one lifetime old. PAYOFF: Granny Burr's husband's notes (Mossgrove) are copies of these carvings. PLANT: the woman in the carvings is the statue in Emberbrook ("She saw us clear"). SHAPE BREAK, THE LOSS (rule 80): after the ring is won, a Council envoy reads an order signed by Orlen Vael himself with a kind note, "for your own protection": the Wand Sanctum is sealed. Before it happens the player is warned and chooses which five hontori to carry (a clear choice screen). Wren is an apprentice of Rootwood now and comes through Stonehollow; she loses hontori to the seal too. The seal lasts until chapter 8.
- Rewards: the third evolution happens around level 20, near Harrow.
- Coins: lantern oil, a miner's rope, Dugsworth treats.

## Chapter 7: Where the Chimney Leads (fire, levels 20 to 23)
- The Ash Road (too hot until Harrow lays a stone bridge for a ring holder: flag `c6:bridge`) leads to Cinderreach, a volcano town. Places: Cinderreach (tavern, shop where Ember Dust is cheap, about 4 houses), the Ash Road, the Hearth Below (a lava cave).
- Small school: the Smoke House teaches Swift Step (a new spell id `step`: escaping becomes much likelier, never certain, rule 33).
- Great school: Ember Crucible, Warden Brasa, Apprentice Kell. Entrance test: cool the lava with water hontori and light the braziers with fire (puzzle type `braziers`: light braziers in an order shown by carvings; water cools lava tiles into a path). Ring rule `heat`: mana does not refill during the fight; Brasa's lead is an evolved fire hontori fought in two phases (a second phase begins at half health with one short line). Drawing twist `burn`: the line fades behind the finger, so draw in one sure stroke (generous). The Flame Ring is the sixth Warden's vote.
- Story: the kid's own hontori came from here: it fled the night the grey cloaks emptied its nest. It chose the kid's chimney because someone once left a light on there: Grandma, a Wonder who hid her wand. In the Hearth Below the empty nest is found (a quiet scene; the hontori remembers, in rhyme). A letter from young Vael to Grandma is in her old wand box (sent by kite from home with Mom's note): they trained together under the first Wonder. PLANT: Grandma's wand still works. Cole and Mira arrive together; Cole has seen the jars now and stands with the kid (a joint scene, no duel). If the hontori has no name yet, this is where it is invited to choose one.
- Coins: Ember Dust cheap, a heat cloak for the road.
